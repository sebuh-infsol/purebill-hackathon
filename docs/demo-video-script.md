# 3-minute demo video — shot script (new work)

Total: ~180 seconds. Film the screen + the staged scene; read the bold
lines verbatim. Every claim on screen maps to a repo file.

## 0:00–0:15 — Hook (repo README on screen)

**"A field tech photographs a repair. Our pipeline counts what's in the
photo, drafts the invoice for human review, and proves every step. Seven
items boxed, four lines proposed, three specs honestly refused."**

## 0:15–0:35 — The locked scene (photo + INVENTORY.md)

Show the staged table. **"Before any photo existed, we locked this
inventory: one fan, four screws, one screwdriver, one cable — seven items
for job HB-1042."** Cut to `INVENTORY.md` and the capture receipt: hash,
68,770 bytes.

## 0:35–1:15 — SAM boxes (infographic fullscreen)

Show `sam-boxes-infographic.jpg`. **"SAM 3.1 boxed the scene in three
passes. The honest part: whole-scene mode mistook four lid magnets for
screws — caught by overlay, fixed by cropping to the tray and re-running.
Four interior boxes, four screws confirmed."** Point at the red Xs, then
the green boxes. **"The cable took the same treatment on a fan-unit crop."**

## 1:15–1:40 — Describe (describe-result.md)

**"The contributor model quotes each box back, confirms seven of seven by
location — and refuses what it can't see: screw size, pin count, labor
time. The refusal is the feature."**

## 1:40–2:20 — Invoice (browser: sandbox invoice 1038)

Show invoice 1038 open. **"Four lines mapped to real sandbox items,
quantities one-one-four-one from the photo. Posted, unsent, forty-one
dollars — sandbox only, production never touched."** Cut to the PDF and
screenshot in the repo.

## 2:20–2:50 — Honesty close (architecture.md)

**"Two agents: Muse Spark sees photos, never credentials. The Hermes
agent holds the books and writes only approved sandbox calls. Prior
platform work is separate prior art. Everything you saw is in the public
repo, hashes and all."**

## 2:50–3:00 — End card

Repo URL on screen. **"PureBill — the photo is evidence, the review is the
product."**
