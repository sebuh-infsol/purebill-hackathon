# 3-minute demo video — shot script v2 (live-footage cut, new work)

Total: ~180 seconds. Three live screen recordings carry the body;
talking head opens/closes; infographics are bridges. Read bold lines verbatim.

## 0:00–0:05 — Title card (workspace pan, dimmed + title)

Slow left-to-right pan: white folding table to monitor desk. Dim ~40%,
title: "PureBill — field photo to invoice lines". Hard cut. Room tone only.

## 0:05–0:15 — Hook (talking head, 10s)

**"A field tech photographs a repair. Our pipeline counts what's in the
photo, drafts the invoice for human review, and proves every step."**

## 0:15–0:25 — Locked scene (photo + receipt flash)

Locked photo fullscreen. **"Before any photo existed, we locked this
inventory: one fan, four screws, one screwdriver, one cable — job HB-1042.
Hash verified on readback."** (~10s, move fast.)

## 0:25–1:00 — LIVE 1: SAM boxes (screen recording, `bash /tmp/live_sam.sh`)

Roll the terminal take. Narrate over the typing pause: **"SAM 3.1,
live — one concept, one call."** When the box prints: **"Screwdriver,
boxed. Every box in this demo was overlay-verified before it touched a
line item."**

## 1:00–1:40 — LIVE 2: contributor describe (screen recording, `bash /tmp/live_describe.sh`)

Roll the take (thinking time reads as live — keep it). When output lands:
**"The contributor model quotes our boxes back, confirms seven of seven by
location — and refuses what it can't see: screw size, pin count, labor
time. The refusal is the feature."**

## 1:40–2:10 — LIVE 3: invoice readback (screen recording, `bash /tmp/live_invoice.sh`)

Roll the Hermes agent take. **"The office agent reads back posted sandbox
invoice 1038: our customer, our four lines, one-one-four-one, forty-one
dollars. Real books, read live — nothing staged."**

## 2:10–2:35 — Validation story (SAM infographic, 25s max)

**"How we earned the boxes: whole-scene mode mistook four lid magnets for
screws — caught by overlay, fixed by cropping to the tray. Four interior
boxes, four screws."** Keep it tight; the live footage already proved it.

## 2:35–2:55 — Handoff + close (talking head + handoff infographic)

**"Two agents: Muse Spark sees photos, never credentials. The Hermes
agent holds the books and writes only approved sandbox calls. Prior
platform work is separate prior art — this repo is all new."**

## 2:55–3:00 — End card

Repo URL fullscreen. **"PureBill — the photo is evidence, the review is
the product."**

## Edit notes

- Live takes first in the timeline; trim their dead air, never their output.
- Infographics are bridges (5–25s), never the body.
- If over 3:00, cut the 2:10 magnet story to one line, never a live take.
- Export 1080p, watch once with earbuds, then submit.
